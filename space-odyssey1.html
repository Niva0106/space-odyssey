Mission instructions

You are the mission design team. Your choices decide whether the spacecraft completes its objectives and makes it home.

  1. Define the objective Review the mission goal, destination and science requirements.
  2. Build your spacecraft Choose instruments and systems while balancing mass, power and budget.
  3. Plan the flight Select a launch vehicle and weigh communication and orbital limits.
  4. Operate the mission Navigate, watch your resources and respond to problems in flight.
  5. Review the outcome See how your engineering decisions shaped the result.

All values are simplified for gameplay and learning. They are not real mission specifications.
